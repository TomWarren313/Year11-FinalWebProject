About Me

My name is Tom, and I am a Year 11 student at Sacred Heart College in Auckland. I enjoy technology and computer science, and I hope to become a software engineer in the future. I like learning how things work, solving problems, and working on projects where I can put my ideas into practice. Outside of class, I am involved in robotics club, table tennis, and Young Vinnies. I enjoy being part of different activities at school because they give me opportunities to learn new things, meet people, and develop skills outside of my normal classes. Through Young Vinnies, I have been involved in community service and helping others, which is something I value. I was also fortunate enough to receive the Catholic Caring Award for my involvement in Young Vinnies. I would describe myself as someone who is curious and enjoys learning about things that interest me. I like a challenge, especially when it involves figuring something out for myself, and I am always looking for ways to improve my skills. I also value being able to work well with others and contribute to the community around me.

Pope Francis: Words of Wisdom

Pope Francis has expressed concerns about how social media and modern technology can affect young people's lives. He has warned that excessive use of digital platforms can create isolation and encourage people to live in an unrealistic online world. In 2016, he told thousands of teenagers that "happiness is not an app you can download", explaining that genuine happiness cannot be achieved through technology, popularity or material possessions. Instead, he encouraged young people to develop meaningful relationships and focus on what truly matters in life.

Pope Francis believes social media should bring people together rather than separate them. He encourages people to use technology positively by showing kindness, understanding and respect towards others online. This connects to the Catholic principle of Human Dignity, which teaches that everyone deserves to be treated with respect, regardless of their background or online presence. His message reminds us that technology should strengthen human relationships rather than replace genuine connections with others.

Are We Scrolling Our Lives Away?

My name is Tom, and I am a Year 11 student at Sacred Heart College in Auckland. I enjoy technology and computer science, and I hope to become a software engineer in the future. I like learning how things work, solving problems, and working on projects where I can put my ideas into practice. Outside of class, I am involved in robotics club, table tennis, and Young Vinnies. I enjoy being part of different activities at school because they give me opportunities to learn new things, meet people, and develop skills outside of my normal classes. Through Young Vinnies, I have been involved in community service and helping others, which is something I value. I was also fortunate enough to receive the Catholic Caring Award for my involvement in Young Vinnies. I would describe myself as someone who is curious and enjoys learning about things that interest me. I like a challenge, especially when it involves figuring something out for myself, and I am always looking for ways to improve my skills. I also value being able to work well with others and contribute to the community around me.
